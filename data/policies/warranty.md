# Limited Hardware Warranty Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

## 1. Standard Limited Warranty Coverage
All branded electronic products sold by our company come with a standard **1-Year Limited Manufacturer Warranty** starting from the original purchase date.

## 2. What Is Covered
* Defects in materials and workmanship under normal consumer use.
* Hardware failures (e.g., power button failure, audio driver failure, charging port malfunction).
* Internal battery degradation if battery capacity drops below **80% of original rated capacity** within the first 12 months.

## 3. What Is NOT Covered
* **Accidental Damage:** Drops, impacts, cracked screens, or physical crushing.
* **Liquid / Water Ingress:** Water damage unless the product carries an explicit IP rating and damage occurred strictly within certified parameters.
* **Unauthorized Modifications:** Devices opened, repaired, or tampered with by unauthorized third-party technicians.
* **Normal Wear & Tear:** Cosmetic scratches, cosmetic fading, or ear cushion peeling.

## 4. CarePlus Extended Warranty (Add-on)
* Customers can purchase an optional **2-Year CarePlus Protection Plan** within 30 days of hardware purchase.
* CarePlus covers up to **2 accidental damage incidents per year** subject to a $25 deductible, and extends hardware defect coverage to 2 full years.

## 5. How to File a Warranty Claim
1. Have your **Order Number** and device **Serial Number (S/N)** ready.
2. Submit a request to the support desk with photos/videos detailing the defect.
3. If approved, we provide a prepaid return label and repair or replace the device within 7 business days.
