# Store Return & Refund Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

## 1. General Return Window
Customers may return most items purchased directly from our store within **30 days** of delivery for a full refund or exchange, provided the item is in its original condition.

## 2. Category-Specific Return Rules
* **Consumer Electronics & Gadgets:** Must be returned within **14 calendar days** from the delivery date. Items must include all original cords, adapters, documentation, and packaging.
* **Open-Box Electronics:** A **15% restocking fee** applies if the packaging seal is broken but all items are intact and undamaged.
* **Apparel & Wearables:** Must be unworn, unwashed, and have original tags attached.
* **Non-Returnable Items:**
  * Gift cards
  * Downloadable software products
  * Clearance / Final Sale items (explicitly marked "Final Sale")
  * Personalized or customized products

## 3. Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Items
* If an item arrives damaged or defective, report it to customer service within **48 hours of delivery**.
* We will provide a prepaid return label and issue a **free replacement** or an immediate **100% full refund** (restocking fees are strictly waived for damaged goods).

## 4. Refund Processing Timelines
* Once our warehouse inspects the returned package (typically 2-3 business days after arrival), the refund is processed.
* **Credit Card & Debit Card:** 5 to 7 business days depending on your bank.
* **Store Credit / Gift Card:** Available immediately upon return approval.
* Original shipping fees are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error or a defective item.
