# Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

## 1. Shipping Methods & Estimated Delivery Times
We offer three shipping options for domestic and international orders:

* **Standard Shipping (3-5 business days):**
  * Free on all domestic orders of $50 or more.
  * Flat fee of $4.99 for orders under $50.
* **Express Delivery (1-2 business days):**
  * Flat fee of $14.99 regardless of order total.
  * Orders placed before 1:00 PM EST ship the same day.
* **International Shipping (7-14 business days):**
  * Available to select countries. Rates calculated at checkout based on weight and destination.
  * Customs duties and taxes may be applied by local authorities and are the customer's responsibility.

## 2. Order Processing & Tracking
* Orders are processed within **24 hours** on business days (Monday through Friday, excluding public holidays).
* Once your package has shipped, a tracking number and courier link will be emailed to you immediately.

## 3. Delayed, Lost, or Stolen Packages
* **Tracking Inactive:** If your tracking status has not updated for more than **5 consecutive days**, please contact customer support.
* **Package Marked Delivered but Not Received:** Check with neighbors, building management, and safe drop-off spots. If still missing after 24 hours, contact customer support to initiate a courier trace.
* **Address Corrections:** Delivery addresses can only be modified within **1 hour** of order placement by contacting live support.
