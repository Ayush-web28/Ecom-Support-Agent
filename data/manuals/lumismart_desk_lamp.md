# LumiSmart LED Desk Lamp (Model LD-200) - User Manual

## 1. Specifications & Dimensions
* **Model Number:** LD-200
* **Luminous Flux (Max Brightness):** 1,000 Lumens
* **Color Temperature Range:** 2700K (Warm Sunset) to 6500K (Cool Daylight)
* **Color Rendering Index (CRI):** Ra ≥ 95 (True-to-life color accuracy)
* **Wireless Charging Base Output:** 15W Qi-certified Fast Wireless Charger (supports iPhone, Samsung Galaxy, AirPods)
* **Input Power:** 24V / 1.5A DC Adapter (Included)
* **Connectivity:** Wi-Fi 802.11 b/g/n (2.4 GHz only; 5 GHz Wi-Fi is NOT supported) + Bluetooth 5.0

## 2. Touch Controls & Timer
* **Power Button:** Tap once to turn ON/OFF.
* **Brightness Slider:** Slide finger left/right along the touch bar to adjust brightness from 10% to 100%.
* **Color Mode Button:** Tap to cycle through 4 presets: Reading (4000K), Computer Work (5000K), Relax (2700K), Creative (6500K).
* **Auto-Shutoff Timer:** Press the Timer button once for 30 minutes (Green LED), twice for 45 minutes (Blue LED), or three times for 60 minutes (Purple LED).

## 3. Smart App Setup & Wi-Fi Connection
1. Download the **"LumiSmart Home"** app from the Apple App Store or Google Play Store.
2. Plug in the lamp. The base LED should blink rapidly in **Blue**, indicating Wi-Fi setup mode.
3. In the app, tap **"Add Device" (+)** and confirm your phone is connected to a **2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network**.
4. Follow the on-screen instructions to finish pairing.
5. Voice Assistant Integration: Link the LumiSmart skill in Amazon Alexa, Google Home, or Apple HomeKit.

## 4. Troubleshooting & Wi-Fi Reset
* **Cannot connect to Wi-Fi:** Ensure your Wi-Fi router broadcasts a distinct 2.4 GHz SSID. Turn off Wi-Fi isolation / guest network mode.
* **Factory / Wi-Fi Reset:**
  1. Turn the lamp ON.
  2. Toggle the main physical power switch on the power cord **OFF and ON 5 times consecutively** (leaving a 1-second interval between switches).
  3. The lamp will flash three times in white and the indicator will start blinking rapid blue, indicating the lamp has returned to factory reset state.
