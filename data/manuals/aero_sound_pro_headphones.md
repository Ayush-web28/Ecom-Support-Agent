# AeroSound Pro Wireless ANC Headphones (Model AP-900) - User Manual

## 1. Specifications & Hardware
* **Model Number:** AP-900
* **Driver Size:** 40mm Custom Dynamic Drivers
* **Frequency Response:** 20 Hz – 40,000 Hz
* **Bluetooth Version:** 5.3 (Operating range: up to 15 meters / 50 feet)
* **Supported Audio Codecs:** AAC, SBC, LDAC (Hi-Res Wireless)
* **Water & Dust Resistance:** IPX4 (Sweat and splash resistant; not submerged in water)
* **Weight:** 255 grams

## 2. Battery & Charging
* **Battery Life (ANC On):** Up to 30 hours of continuous playback.
* **Battery Life (ANC Off):** Up to 40 hours of continuous playback.
* **Fast Charging:** A 10-minute charge via USB-C provides up to 5 hours of listening time.
* **Full Recharge Time:** Approximately 90 minutes from 0% to 100%.

## 3. Bluetooth Pairing Instructions
1. Ensure the headphones are powered off.
2. Press and hold the **Power Button for 5 seconds** until the LED indicator flashes alternating **Red and Blue**, indicating pairing mode.
3. On your smartphone, tablet, or laptop, enable Bluetooth and select **"AeroSound Pro"** from the discovered devices list.
4. The LED will illuminate solid Blue for 3 seconds once paired.

## 4. Multipoint Connection (Dual Device Pairing)
* AeroSound Pro can connect to two Bluetooth devices simultaneously (e.g., phone and laptop).
* After pairing the first device, turn off Bluetooth on that device, re-enter pairing mode, and connect the second device. Then re-enable Bluetooth on the first device. The headphones will connect to both.

## 5. Factory Reset Procedure
If experiencing audio dropouts, connectivity issues, or button unresponsiveness:
1. Power on the headphones.
2. Press and hold both the **Volume Up (+)** and **Volume Down (-)** buttons simultaneously for **7 seconds**.
3. The LED indicator will flash **Pink three times**, indicating the device has been reset to factory defaults. All prior Bluetooth pairings will be cleared.
